# Iteration 11 Warrior Momentum and Wizard Nuke

Give Warrior and Wizard a class action alongside their current attack and Run. Warrior gains Momentum. Wizard gains Nuke. Rogue keeps Melee, Exploit, and Run.

## Approach

Reuse the encounter's rolled Turn Order for both new actions. Resolving Nuke as soon as the button is tapped would let a slower Wizard act before the slime. The owner confirmed that Nuke resolves on the Wizard's turn. Both new actions replace the class's ordinary attack for that round and may be chosen regardless of which combatant goes first.

## Warrior Momentum

- On the Warrior's turn, Momentum adds exactly one Momentum stack and deals no damage. It requires no roll.
- Each stack adds one die to the Warrior's Melee attack pool and one die to the Warrior's Defense pool. The stacks apply immediately. A slime acting later in the Momentum round faces the boosted defense. A slime acting earlier faces the defense the Warrior had at the start of the round.
- Melee and Defense rolls keep their existing dice and damage rules. Using either roll leaves all Momentum stacks in place.
- There is no gameplay cap on Momentum stacks. Individual Melee and Defense rolls remain subject to the existing 1,024-dice ceiling. A new encounter starts at zero stacks. Stacks clear on victory, defeat, or escape.

## Wizard Nuke

- On the Wizard's turn, Nuke deals damage equal to effective Cunning + Lore, including advancement, straight to the target's Health. Neither side rolls for the Nuke and the target's Defense does not reduce it.
- If the slime acts first and defeats the Wizard, Nuke never fires. A lethal Nuke prevents a later slime attack and triggers the existing victory, XP, and healing flow. A surviving slime takes its normal turn.
- Nuke has no resource cost or cooldown in this iteration.

## Display and saved games

- Show Momentum for Warriors and Nuke for Wizards on the combat screen with the same keyboard and touch protections as other actions. Keep Turn Order visible. Show the Warrior's current Momentum stacks and boosted Melee and Defense pools. Show the Wizard's current Nuke damage. Keep the class action labels and descriptions visible on the appropriate character sheets.
- Describe Momentum and Nuke in the round summary and live announcement in resolution order. Roll details show the dice that were actually rolled and explain the new actions without fabricating an attack or defense roll for them.
- Keep roster v10 and checkpoint v9. Momentum and the combat log live only in the encounter. Existing characters gain the action for their class automatically.

## Acceptance

Verify Momentum accumulation, the earlier and later defense rolls in initiative order, persistent bonuses across Melee rounds, and a fresh encounter after escape or Restart. Verify Nuke damage with advancement, no Nuke dice, lethal Nuke skipping a later slime turn, and an earlier slime defeat preventing Nuke. Exercise class restrictions and repeated input, all three classes, existing saves, and the supported phone viewports.
